  • Effective armorA shell pens when it beats plate ÷ cos(angle). Past 70° AP and APCR bounce. The reticle turns green, yellow or red for the plate under it.
  • AmmoAP is the standard round. APCR has 25% more pen and flies faster but loses pen at range. HE never bounces, wrecks modules and splashes damage through thin armor even without a pen.
  • View rangeEach vehicle spots enemies only inside its view circle: Light 114 m, Medium and Sniper 90 m, Heavy 71 m, SPG 65 m. Your team shares what anyone sees.
  • Sight linesInside that range, an enemy is visible when anyone on your team has a line to it that no building, wall, rock, bush, tree or smoke cuts. A bush or tree you sit in doesn't block your own view.
  • ProximityWithin 15 m (the small dotted ring around you) every enemy is spotted automatically, even in a bush or around the corner of a building.
  • FiringFiring reveals you for 4 s to every enemy within 1.5× its view range unless a rock or building is in between. Bushes don't hide a muzzle flash. Exception: if you're unspotted and inside a bush, firing only leaves a sound ring. Repeat shots tighten it. Being hit also shows your HP marker for 4 s.
  • Artillery splashA shell lands at an angle and throws its blast forward along its flight. Close shots fly flat and throw a long cone ahead of the impact. Long shots are lobbed high, come down steeply and splash almost evenly all round. Flight time runs from 0.1 s point blank to 2 s at 225 m. Shells hit rocks, walls and buildings in their path: low, close shots are stopped by cover, high lobs clear it. The reticle marks a blocked shot with a red X. Splash damage falls off evenly from full at the impact to zero at the edge of that shape. Only a direct hit counts as a penetration in your battle stats. Splash is listed separately. The SPG reticle shows the exact shape: solid for damage, dashed for stun.
  • Rocket artilleryThe Tempest and Cyclone (rocket branch off the Howler, tiers II and III) fire a salvo of 12 or 16 rockets that scatter over a wide circle around your aim point, then reload for 20 s (Tempest) or 25 s (Cyclone). Each rocket has its own blast and stuns like a full shell. Rockets fly twice as long as a howitzer shell (up to 4 s), so their landing zones give enemies time to drive out. The artillery line continues from the Howler with heavier howitzers (Thunder II, Colossus III); the rockets are a side branch off the Howler. A salvo usually lands several hits on anything inside the circle and softens every tank near it.
  • Tech treeEvery line has three tiers. Battles earn XP for the vehicle you drove; enough XP researches the next vehicle in that line (2,500 XP for tier II, 6,000 for tier III), and credits buy it (12,000 and 30,000). Bots in your battles come from your tier, sometimes one above or below.
  • Training groundPick Training as the battle mode to practise with your own vehicles, or press Test drive on any vehicle in the Tech tree to try it once, stock, without buying it. Stationary targets of every class never move or shoot but still spot you. Each hit you land is explained on screen: plate, angle, effective armor, your pen at that range and the damage. Ammo is unlimited, destroyed targets come back after 4 s, and nothing is earned.
  • Incoming fireThe moment an enemy SPG fires, the landing zone of its shell shows on the ground in red, and the inner area fills up as impact gets closer. Drive out of it. A whistle plays when it is aimed within 45 m of you.
  • StunArtillery shells stun every enemy inside a zone of the same shape, 1.75× larger, for up to 12 s: slower drive, turn, traverse and reload. Damage your team deals to a tank you stunned counts as your stun assist.
  • ConsumablesRepair kit fixes tracks, gun and engine and shakes off stun. Extinguisher puts out fire and fireproofs you for 12 s. Smoke drops a 14 s cloud that blocks sight lines.
  • BasesStandard: capture the enemy base or destroy every tank. Encounter: one neutral base. Hitting a capturer resets its share of the points.
  • TerrainTrees fall when you drive into them. Shallow fords and marsh slow you. Deep river water is impassable. Ram enemies at speed for damage. Heavier tanks win.
  • ViewsTactical 3D looks down with north up. Chase puts the camera behind your hull. Classic 2D is the top-down map. Switch with V or the view button under the timer.
  • ControlsDesktop: WASD drive, mouse aims, click fires, 1–3 ammo, 4–6 consumables, Shift (or Z) switches sniper mode on and off, or hold the right mouse button. In sniper mode the cursor reaches your gun's full range at the edge of the screen, Esc pauses. Reversing steers like a car backing up: S+A swings the rear left. Phone: left thumb drives, right thumb drags the aim and fires on release; drag back to the middle of the stick before lifting to cancel the shot. Artillery never fires on lift: aim, let go, then press either red FIRE button (left or right edge). Aim stays where you left it; if it was off screen it comes back to normal range. For guns, the scope zoom drops back to normal on release. Tap the minimap to send allies there.
