  • Effective armorA shell pens when it beats plate ÷ cos(angle). Past 70° AP and APCR bounce. The reticle turns green, yellow or red for the plate under it.
  • AmmoAP is the standard round. APCR has 25% more pen and flies faster but loses pen at range. HE never bounces, wrecks modules and splashes damage through thin armor even without a pen.
  • View rangeEach vehicle spots enemies only inside its view circle: Light 760 m, Medium and Sniper 600 m, Heavy 470 m, SPG 430 m. Your team shares what anyone sees.
  • Sight linesInside that range, an enemy is visible when anyone on your team has a line to it that no bush, tree or smoke cuts. Cover you sit in doesn't block your own view. Within 30 m everyone is seen.
  • Markers and soundA tank's HP marker shows when it is struck or fires while seen. A hidden tank that fires only leaves a sound ring. Repeat shots tighten it.
  • StunArtillery shells stun every enemy in a ring wider than the blast for up to 12 s: slower drive, turn, traverse and reload. Damage your team deals to a tank you stunned counts as your stun assist.
  • ConsumablesRepair kit fixes tracks, gun and engine and shakes off stun. Extinguisher puts out fire and fireproofs you for 12 s. Smoke drops a 14 s cloud that blocks sight lines.
  • BasesStandard: capture the enemy base or destroy every tank. Encounter: one neutral base. Hitting a capturer resets its share of the points.
  • TerrainTrees fall when you drive into them. Shallow fords and marsh slow you. Deep river water is impassable. Ram enemies at speed for damage. Heavier tanks win.
  • ViewsTactical 3D looks down with north up. Chase puts the camera behind your hull. Classic 2D is the top-down map. Switch with V or the view button under the timer.
  • ControlsDesktop: WASD drive, mouse aims, click fires, 1–3 ammo, 4–6 consumables, hold Shift to scope, Esc pauses. Phone: left thumb drives, right thumb nudges the aim and fires on release. Tap the minimap to send allies there.
